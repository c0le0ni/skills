# Privacy

| Data | Why it is collected | Where it lives | Who sees it | How long it stays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer name | To call the order at the counter | App database | Counter staff, Nora | 90 days after pickup, *pending ADR-0004* |
| WhatsApp number | Order confirmation and "ready" message | App database, messaging provider | Counter staff, Nora | 90 days after pickup, *pending ADR-0004* |
| Order notes | Cake message and special requests | App database | Counter staff, Nora | With the order |

- Legal basis: contract (the customer places an order).
- No marketing messages without separate consent.
- How a customer asks to see or delete their data: by WhatsApp to the shop number.
