# Juniper Bakery: briefing (fictional example)

Form answers sent by Nora Lindqvist, owner, plus notes from a 20-minute call.

## Form answers

**What do you want to build?**
An app like the big delivery platforms, where customers order, pay, track the
driver, earn points, leave reviews. Also a website. Maybe a page for wholesale
clients (cafés that buy from us).

**What do you sell?**
Sourdough, pastries, cakes to order. About 40 products, 12 of them every day.
Cakes need 48h notice.

**How do customers order today?**
Mostly WhatsApp. Some by phone, a few walk in. On Saturdays we get 60 to 90
messages before 10am.

**What went wrong recently?**
Last Saturday we forgot two cake orders that were buried in the chat. One was
for a birthday. We refunded and lost the customer. This happens every few
weeks. Also people ask "is the cardamom bun available today?" all day long.

**What tools do you already use?**
- Square for the card machine and payments in the shop.
- Instagram for photos and promotions (8k followers).
- A Google Sheet with wholesale clients and their weekly standing orders. It
  works fine.
- Our accountant uses Square reports.

**Who will use it?**
Me and two staff at the counter. Customers on their phones.

**What is a must have?**
Customers can order ahead and pick up. We see all orders for the day in one
place. Cakes with the date they are needed.

**Nice to have?**
Loyalty points, delivery with our own driver, reviews, gift cards, wholesale
portal, an app in the stores.

**Deadline?**
Before the holiday season, so in 7 weeks.

**Budget?**
Not sure yet. Small.

## Call notes

- Nora repeated the forgotten cakes story twice. Clearly the main pain.
- Delivery: "maybe next year, we only have one car".
- Payments: she would like online payment "eventually"; today customers pay at
  pickup and that is fine for now.
- Asked about loyalty: they have a paper stamp card that "works ok".
- Wholesale: cafés send orders by email on Mondays, Nora copies to the Sheet.
  "Not a problem."
- She wants customers to see which products are available today.
- Menu photos: "the photographer sent some, I'll forward them" (not received).
- Domain: thinks they own juniperbakery.com, not sure who registered it.
- Two staff are not very techy, need something simple on a tablet.
