# Variations: Today’s menu card

The card next to the headline is the first thing a customer reads: what’s ready, when, how much, and what’s gone. Three directions, all with the bakery’s own colors and fonts, none adding a fact that isn’t already on the page.

0. **Current**
1. **Receipt**: A printed ticket from the counter: monospace, dotted leaders to the price, a torn edge. Feels made today.
2. **Chalkboard**: The board behind the counter, in the brand’s sage: big serif names, prices in terracotta, the time as a small chip. Strong next to the cream page.
3. **Tiles**: One tile per item, the ready time on top, and a last tile for the cakes the page already sells. Scans fastest on a phone and makes “sold out” obvious, at the cost of the list’s calm.
