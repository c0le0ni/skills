What to fix most important first

  • Looks like a button, isn't oneFix first

    div.send looks like a button but is a div with a text cursor: it can't be focused, ignores Enter and screen readers don't announce it.

    How to fixUse <button type="submit"> (or <a href> if it navigates) with the same style.
    /coleoni-polish
  • “Inter” only shows for people who have it installedFix first

    The CSS asks for Inter with no @font-face. It's installed on this machine, so it looks right here; most visitors get the next font in the stack.

    How to fixServe it (woff2 from your own domain, only the weights in use, font-display: swap) or drop it from the stack and use the system font on purpose.
    /coleoni-type
  • Contrast 2.12:1 on “With cream cheese and toaste”Fix first

    #A9AFA4 on #FBF8F2, 14px. Needs 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).

    How to fixThe same color a little darker: #6D7368 (4.63:1).
    /coleoni-color
  • Contrast 2.91:1 on “Six flavors, three sizes and”Fix first

    #8B8F87 on #F6F0E6, 18px. Needs 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).

    How to fixThe same color a little darker: #6B6F67 (4.55:1).
    /coleoni-color
  • Contrast 2.92:1 on “Priya S., ordered a carrot c”Fix first

    #8A8F86 on #F6F0E6, 13.5px. Needs 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).

    How to fixThe same color a little darker: #6A6F66 (4.57:1).
    /coleoni-color
  • 5 more pairs with low contrastFix first

    3.12 · 3.34 · 3.40 · 3.71 · 3.93

    How to fixSee the contrast table below.
    /coleoni-color
  • Scrolls sideways at 390pxFix first

    166px wider than the screen, starting at article.card.

    How to fixColumns with repeat(auto-fill, minmax(min(100%, 16rem), 1fr)), min-width: 0 on flex children, tables inside an overflow-x: auto wrapper.
    /coleoni-layout
  • 7 different corner radiiFix

    In use: 4px ×3, 6px ×1, 8px ×1, 10px ×1, 12px ×4, 16px ×1, 20px ×1. Close values (10, 12, 16) read as mistakes, not choices.

    How to fixA scale of 3 or 4 tokens (--r-sm, --r-md, --r-lg, --r-pill), and each component picks one.
    /coleoni-polish
  • Inner corner doesn't follow the outer oneFix

    An element with a 12px radius inside a 16px one, 10px from its edge. The corners aren't parallel and the inner one looks swollen.

    How to fixInner radius = outer radius − the gap: 16 − 10 = 6px, or calc(var(--r-lg) - var(--pad)).
    /coleoni-polish
  • A hard, black shadowFix

    rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5) 0px 2px 4px 0px: black at high opacity with little blur reads as a dirty edge, not depth.

    How to fixMore blur, low opacity (6 to 15%) and the brand's dark color instead of black.
    /coleoni-polish
  • 15 spacings off the 4px gridFix

    Values like 11px ×7, 5px ×6, 26px ×5, 13px ×4, 22px ×4, 23px ×4, 14px ×3, 18px ×3. Almost equal to their neighbors on the scale, they create 1 to 3px misalignments.

    How to fixA spacing scale in multiples of 4 (4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64) and tokens for it.
    /coleoni-polish
  • 3 kinds of button that don't matchFix

    Height, radius and font size: 46px · 6px · 15.5px, 46px · pill · 14px, 46px · 8px · 15px.

    How to fixOne button component with color variants (primary, secondary) and at most two sizes, same height and radius.
    /coleoni-polish
  • 2 buttons with no hover stateFix

    Nothing changes when the pointer is over them: they feel dead.

    How to fixA subtle :hover (background 6 to 10% darker), :active 1px down, and a 150ms transition on the properties that change.
    /coleoni-polish
  • Icon off the text's centerFix

    The icon sits 3.2px below the center of the text line.

    How to fixRemove manual margins on the icon and align with display:inline-flex; align-items:center on the button.
    /coleoni-polish
  • 13 font sizes, 7 almost equalFix

    In use: 12 13 13.5 14 15 15.5 17 18 21 22 23 28 46. Pairs nobody can tell apart: 13/13.5, 13.5/14, 14/15, 15/15.5, 17/18, 21/22, 22/23.

    How to fixA scale of 5 to 7 steps with a steady ratio (1.2 to 1.33), as rem tokens: for example 13 · 15 · 17 · 21 · 28 · 44.
    /coleoni-type
  • Lines too longFix

    About 130 characters per line. Past 75, reading tires and the eye misses the next line.

    How to fixmax-width: 34em (about 65 characters) on paragraphs.
    /coleoni-type
  • 3 groups of almost identical colorsFix

    #2F4A3A ×7 #2E4B3B ×1 #304A39 ×1
    #8A8F86 ×5 #8B8F87 ×1
    #C8643B ×4 #C9663C ×1
    Nobody sees the difference; the code keeps both forever.

    How to fixKeep the most used one in each group and make it a token.
    /coleoni-color
  • 34 colors written straight into the CSSFix

    34 of 34 color declarations use raw values instead of variables (0 use tokens). Changing the brand becomes a treasure hunt.

    How to fixSemantic tokens (--bg, --text, --accent…) on top of a scale; see palette.css.
    /coleoni-color
  • 2 edges that almost line upFix

    30px instead of 32px (-2px, 3 blocks)
    34px instead of 30px (+4px, 3 blocks)
    Nobody notices the number; everyone feels the page is crooked.

    How to fixOne container (max-width + margin-inline: auto + one padding-inline token) for every section, instead of padding per section.
    /coleoni-layout
  • No rhythm between sectionsFix

    Gaps between sections: 72px → 58px → 64px → 66px. Values this close look like mistakes.

    How to fixOne section spacing token (for example --s-8: 64px), and a bigger one only where the subject really changes.
    /coleoni-layout

  • No skip linkWorth a look

    The first Tab stop is not a link to the main content, so keyboard users go through the whole menu on every page. WCAG 2.4.1

    How to fixAdd “Skip to content” as the first focusable element, pointing to <main id="content">. eMAG 1.5 asks for it.
    /coleoni-a11y
