# Juniper Bakery: brand notes (fictional example)

Sent by Nora with the photographer's mood board.

- Paper bags are cream (#F6F0E6) with the logo in deep sage (#2F4A3A).
- Price tags and stickers use a terracotta orange (#C8643B), only for small things.
- The shop sign uses a rounded serif, "Fraunces" according to the designer.
- Menu boards and the receipts use "Inter".
- Photos: warm light, wooden counter, never on white background.
- Nora: "we are not a fancy pâtisserie, we are the bakery of the street".
