Today’s menu card

The card next to the headline is the first thing a customer reads: what’s ready, when, how much, and what’s gone. Three directions, all with the bakery’s own colors and fonts, none adding a fact that isn’t already on the page.

Pick one: 1, 2 or 3, or mix (“2 with the prices of 3”).
